01 / THE FIGHTER FSM

Mermaid source
flowchart TD
  READY["IDLE / WALKING"] -->|E or R|STARTUP["STARTUP<br/>Attack windup"]
  STARTUP -->|Spec startup|ACTIVE["ACTIVE<br/>May deal damage"]
  ACTIVE -->|Spec active|RECOVERY["RECOVERY<br/>Movement locked"]
  RECOVERY -->|Full recovery|READY
  RECOVERY -->|Valid R follow-up|STARTUP
Mobile Mermaid source
flowchart TD
  READY["IDLE / WALKING"] -->|E or R|STARTUP["STARTUP<br/>Attack windup"]
  STARTUP -->|Spec startup|ACTIVE["ACTIVE<br/>May deal damage"]
  ACTIVE -->|Spec active|RECOVERY["RECOVERY<br/>Movement locked"]
  RECOVERY -->|Allowed transition|NEXT["IDLE / WALKING<br/>or combo STARTUP"]

Punches, kicks and mosquito stings share startup → active → recovery. The selected AttackSpec supplies their timing. Damage can interrupt any living state; the victim then enters Hurt or Dead (see hit reactions below).

E and R each queue one attack request for 160 ms. Idle or walking consumes it immediately; otherwise it waits until an allowed transition and expires if that takes too long. Key repeats do not queue attacks. A hit clears the victim's existing buffer and combo; a fresh request made during Hurt can still buffer.

Movement and facing are locked during attacks. Recovery normally completes in full. The exception is a confirmed, buffered R follow-up at the permitted combo transition.

02 / R → R → R

Mermaid source
flowchart TD
  FRONT["FRONT KICK<br/>1 damage / push"] -->|Confirmed R|HIGH["HIGH KICK<br/>2 damage / launch"]
  HIGH -->|Confirmed R|CRITICAL["VERY HIGH KICK<br/>3 damage / critical"]
  CRITICAL -->|Full recovery|RESET["CHAIN ENDS<br/>Next R is front"]

Front kick must hit a surviving enemy to confirm High. High must then hit that same enemy to confirm Very high. Each confirmation lasts 420 ms from contact. A follow-up also needs a fresh R request still inside its 160 ms input buffer.

Front can transition after 80 ms of recovery; High after 100 ms of recovery. Startup and Active still run normally. Starting the next stage consumes its confirmation, so a missed High cannot reuse the old Front hit.

Miss, timeout, receiving damage, or starting Punch resets the chain. An early request can expire before the transition; holding R cannot play the whole combo. Without a valid follow-up, the normal recovery applies.

Mosquitoes have 6 health by default: the full 1 + 2 + 3 chain kills one. A surviving critical victim shows a white stun marker. A swing may hit multiple enemies, but the chain tracks the first surviving victim hit.

03 / WHO GETS HURT?

Mermaid source
flowchart TD
  ACTIVE["ATTACKER ACTIVE"] -->|Test target|CHECK["Facing / range / lane<br/>Height / vulnerability"]
  CHECK -->|All checks pass|DAMAGE["APPLY DAMAGE<br/>Push / launch / stun"]
  DAMAGE -->|Health remains|HURT["HURT<br/>Attack interrupted"]
  DAMAGE -->|Health is zero|DEAD["DEAD<br/>Terminal state"]
  HURT -->|Stun over + landed|READY["IDLE<br/>Can act again"]
  DEAD -->|Mosquito / 560 ms|HIDDEN["BURST FINISHED<br/>Hide mosquito"]
Mobile Mermaid source
flowchart TD
  ACTIVE["ATTACKER ACTIVE"] -->|Test target|CHECK["Facing / range / lane<br/>Height / vulnerability"]
  CHECK -->|All checks pass|DAMAGE["APPLY DAMAGE<br/>Push / launch / stun"]
  DAMAGE --> REACTION["HURT if still alive<br/>DEAD if health zero"]
  REACTION --> RULES["HURT: stun + landing<br/>DEAD: never recovers"]

CombatLogicService._canHit decides contact, not sprite overlap or an animation event. The target must be alive, not invulnerable, not already hit by this swing, in front and within reach, within 10 px of the attacker's ground lane, and inside the attack's height tolerance. Failed checks cause no damage; contact can still succeed later in the Active phase.

Reach / height tolerance: Punch 32 / 32 px, Low 34 / 32, Frontal 38 / 48, High 42 / 64, Sting 27 / 10. Reach uses fighter center distances. Kick ranges are 4 px shorter than the original values; original 12 / 5 / 3 px knockback is preserved. The full combo connects from normal 23-25 px enemy engagement spacing. Each attack may damage each target once. Player attacks resolve first on simultaneous contact.

_hit applies damage, push, optional launch, stun, and invulnerability (Jaan: 700 ms; mosquitoes: 280 ms). It clears the victim's buffer and combo, then enters Hurt or Dead. The victim's next rendered pose follows that new state.

Mosquito Hurt: five recoil frames play once over 250 ms using stateTime. The final pose holds through longer stun or launch. A new accepted hit resets the clock and restarts recoil. Only when the stun has elapsed and the victim has landed can it return to Idle.

Mosquito Dead: seven burst frames play once over 560 ms, then the mosquito is hidden. Dead remains terminal. won records zero-health enemies; endingAnimationsFinished checks that deaths, attacks, recoil and airborne motion have settled. Both victory and Jaan's defeat now wait for animations and a result popup before Score (see level ending below).

These durations come from combat state time. Finishing a sprite sheet never makes a fighter ready or advances the level.

04 / JUMP IS MOTION, NOT AN ATTACK STATE

Mermaid source
flowchart TD
  SPACE["SPACE REQUEST"] -->|Idle or walking|TAKEOFF["NOT AIRBORNE?<br/>Start jump if yes"]
  TAKEOFF -->|135 px/s upward|AIR["ELEVATION + VELOCITY<br/>Separate from FSM"]
  AIR -->|Gravity each step|LAND["ELEVATION ZERO<br/>Clamp to ground"]

Space is a one-shot request, not an attack buffer. It starts a jump only while Jaan is idle/walking and not already airborne. No double jump and no jumping out of attack recovery. Movement and attacks can still begin in the air when the FSM permits them.

The simulation applies 450 px/s² gravity. Jaan's jump lasts about 600 ms and peaks around 20 px. Ground X/Y, depth sorting, the shadow and camera anchoring stay on the street; only the drawn body moves upward.

Attacks compare both fighters' elevation. A sting only reaches within 10 px of height difference, so it can miss near Jaan's apex while takeoff and landing remain vulnerable. Enemy launches reuse the same gravity and elevation without becoming player jumps.

05 / ONE FRAME: RULES FIRST, SPRITES AFTER

Mermaid source
flowchart TD
  INPUT["InputService<br/>Input intent"] --> GAME["KuradiSaased.update<br/>Fixed 120 Hz steps"]
  GAME --> COMBAT["CombatLogicService<br/>Motion / FSM / hits"]
  COMBAT --> MODEL["FighterModel<br/>State + stateTime"]
  MODEL --> HANDLER["FighterAnimationHandler<br/>Select matching pose"]
  HANDLER --> ENTITY["FighterEntity.render<br/>Draw sprite + elevation"]

For each 1/120-second simulation step, combat consumes intent, updates invulnerability/input/combo timers, advances elevation and FSM phases, starts permitted player attacks or movement, updates mosquito AI, then resolves active attacks in player-first order.

Flame's component update follows the simulation steps. FighterEntity.update copies ground position and depth order. During render, FighterAnimationHandler.frame reads state and state time to choose a pose, and FighterEntity.render draws it at the simulated elevation.

Each kick has its own sheet. Startup shows preparation; Active selects the extended-leg impact pose; Recovery shows retraction. Hurt/death sheets read the victim's state clock. Idle/walk/flight loops may use a decorative elapsed clock; none of those loops can decide hits or readiness.

Accepted takeoff, player attack start and actual contact also emit CombatEvent facts. After each simulation step, GameAudioService routes these to jump, swing, impact and hurt sounds. A separate walking loop follows actual ground movement. Audio completion never changes fighters.

One combat authority: input → simulation → model → presentation. There is no animation-completion callback that changes the FSM.

06 / LET THE ENDING PLAY

Mermaid source
flowchart TD
  OUTCOME["OUTCOME LOCKED<br/>Win or defeat"] --> SETTLE["SETTLE ANIMATIONS<br/>Input and AI stop"]
  SETTLE -->|900 ms + settled|POPUP["RESULT POPUP<br/>Win or defeat text"]
  POPUP -->|1600 ms|SCORE["SCORE VIEW<br/>Record result once"]

The first terminal outcome is latched in LevelEndingModel. New player actions, AI movement, and damage stop; the simulation still advances existing FSM phases and gravity. Victory or defeat cannot change during this settlement.

Jaan's seven death frames play once over 840 ms, without blinking or squashing. He remains lying on the street on the last frame. Mosquito bursts take 560 ms. Attacks, hurt phases and airborne motion also finish before the popup.

The ending waits at least 900 ms and until endingAnimationsFinished. Then a viewport popup says YOU WON! or JAAN IS DOWN for 1600 ms, with combat frozen, before recording the latched result once and entering Score. Enter and attack inputs cannot skip this sequence. Pause freezes its clock; each new level resets it.

These are level-ending phases, separate from the per-fighter combat FSM and the global Menu / Loading / Level / Score / End states. KuradiSaased.update advances them on the fixed simulation clock. The popup only renders them.
